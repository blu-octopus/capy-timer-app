# Capy Timer privacy policy (draft)

This is a draft for hosting before App Store / Play submission. It is not live until you publish it at a public URL and paste that URL into App Store Connect and Play Console.

**Last updated:** 11 August 2026

Capy Timer is a local focus timer. We do not run an account system and we do not operate our own analytics or ad network.

## What stays on your device

- Timer settings, coin balance, unlocked companions, and session history (SQLite + on-device storage).
- Optional local notifications that fire at phase boundaries. These are scheduled on the device; we do not send remote push.
- Home screen widgets read a small snapshot of the current session that the app writes into an App Group / local storage.

## Purchases

If you buy coin packs, Apple or Google process the payment. RevenueCat is used to confirm those purchases with the stores. We do not see your full payment details. Coin packs are consumable: the balance is stored on this device and is not restored onto a new phone.

## What we do not collect

- No sign-in, email, or profile.
- No location, contacts, photos, or microphone.
- No third-party advertising identifier use.

## Children

The app is a general-audience timer. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the support email listed on the store listing (set this when you submit).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
